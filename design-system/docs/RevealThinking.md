# RevealThinking

The back (waiting) face of a flip card.

A big mascot glyph over a line of white serif text. Use this rather than
`MascotPrompt` on the dark face — `MascotPrompt`'s ink text disappears against
the green.

```tsx
<FlipCard back={<RevealThinking>Reading your food…</RevealThinking>} />
```
